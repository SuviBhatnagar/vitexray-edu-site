Curriculum

What you will learn

Six modules that follow how validation work is actually done on automotive programs.

Module 1

Model-based design

MATLAB and Simulink fundamentals, Stateflow, plant models and how models turn into testable software.

Module 2

MIL, SIL & HIL

What each test level proves, when to use it, and how a HIL setup closes the loop around an ECU.

Module 3

Vehicle networks & diagnostics

CAN, CAN-FD and LIN signals and frames, UDS services, and analysis with CANoe and CANalyzer.

Module 4

Test design & execution

Requirements to test cases, test sequences, defect reporting and traceability.

Module 5

Python test automation

Scripting repeatable tests, handling logs and results, and automating common validation tasks.

Module 6

ADAS & EV validation

Typical validation scenarios for driver-assistance features and electric-vehicle systems.

Career readiness, built in

Resume building, mock interviews and job-search guidance run alongside the technical modules. We also include a short theory overview of the dSPACE HIL toolchain, so you understand the terminology employers use.

FAQ

Common questions

Who can join the classes?

College students and OPT, STEM OPT and H1B candidates. If you are in another situation, message us and we will tell you whether the classes fit.

Are the classes online?

Yes. Classes are live and instructor-led, and attended online.

Do I need prior HIL or automotive experience?

No. The classes start from the fundamentals. A background in electronics, electrical, mechanical, computer science or a related engineering field helps.

Will you help me find a job?

Yes. After classes we help with your resume, interview preparation, and finding and applying for roles. We cannot guarantee employment, as hiring decisions are made by employers.

Is the training related to any tool vendor?

No. Vitexray Edu is an independent training provider. We include a short theory overview of the dSPACE HIL toolchain so you understand common industry terminology, but we are not affiliated with or endorsed by any vendor.

When is the next batch, and what does it cost?

New batches open regularly. Contact us for current batch dates, schedule and fees.

Does work authorization affect job placement?

Classes are open to the groups listed above. Job eligibility depends on each candidate's work authorization and on employer requirements, so we discuss this with you up front.
